feat(family): a yellow carnation

A Yellow Carnation

So failing and falling apart; Life makes no sense to me, If I can't find success later- God forbid the curse of being a failure, Definitely kills me inside completely. But is it really my fault? That despite after everything At last, my people misunderstand me so well- And never for once they ever Will resonate the voices that my mind says. The girl who once promised joy now wears The badge of failure, shame, and pain, Labeled selfish, arrogant, and heartless— A reflection of the love that's lost, A family tie that's frayed. 'ONLY THE WISE UNDERSTAND THE UNFATHOMABLE THINGS OF A SOUL' They said. So where are my people? When I wanted Their hand holding? Their love and affection- Where are my OWN BLOODED PEOPLE? Who carry the paradoxical pride of owning me Yet abandon me at the times of need?

— Susmita Jena

feat(peace): dried roses and peace

Dried Roses and Peace

Bittersweet emotions within - Yet here you are sanguine, Choosing love over everytime Representing as the best crime. There's a lot of ambiguity in me However, the thoughts are free! Nourished ones live in hell While the quiet finds home in a cockleshell. You dare ask me what life is to you? And I am already talking through my eyes; you know who. An extraordinary gift of the Almighty 'Or a curse in disguise', I said wisely. Whatever you ponder upon You and I are going to wait till dawn- To witness everything as a tainted masterpiece Silently opting for dried roses and peace.

— Susmita Jena

feat(home): initial commit

Initial Commit

Berhampur taught me that heat has a texture — thick enough to lean on, loud with cicadas arguing about nothing. I left in pieces, the way everyone does, and kept committing small changes to myself ever since: a haircut, a new city, a language I'm still debugging. But some functions never got refactored. I still flinch at loud doors. I still call it home without meaning to.

— Susmita Jena
